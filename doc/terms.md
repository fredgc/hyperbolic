## 3. Mathematical & Numerical Precision Notice

All non-Euclidean transformations, conformal mappings, and boundary circle
inversions are computed numerically using finite floating-point arithmetic:

* Objects near the ideal boundary of the Poincarédisk approach infinite metric
  distortion and may exhibit visual artifacts or rendering clipping due to
  numerical precision limits.

* No representations are made regarding the suitability of the software for
  formal geometric proof verification.
