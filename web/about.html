About Hyperbolic (v{{ VERSION }})

The Hyperbolic Drawing Tool is an interactive application designed to construct, visualize, and explore geometric diagrams in the Poincarédisk model of hyperbolic geometry.

It helps you perform classical straightedge-and-compass constructions adapted to the Poincarédisk (𝔻={z∈ℂ:|z|<1}\mathbb{D} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}), where Euclid’s fifth postulate (the parallel postulate) does not hold.

This application is written in Dart with Flutter—the source code is available on GitHub at [https://github.com/fredgc/hyperbolic][github].

Found a bug or have a feature request? [File an issue on GitHub][issues].

Other apps and drawing tools can be found at [www.gchouse.org][gchouse].

Privacy and Data Retention

This program allows you to customize settings that are saved locally on your device (using SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). No personal data is collected.

On Android, local settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. On Web, settings are deleted when you clear your browser’s local application data.

Drawings may be downloaded to your local device or saved to your Google Drive account. You may also load drawings shared by others, but no files are sent to the gchouse.org server.
